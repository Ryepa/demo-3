FAQ

Good questions

Can't see yours? Ask in the form and we'll answer when we call.

How quickly can you come out?

Most jobs are booked within two working days, and we keep a few same-day slots for urgent problems like leaks.

Do I pay for the quote?

No. Quotes over the phone are free. If a job needs a site visit to price, we'll tell you before booking it.

Do you bring the parts?

We carry common parts like washers, fittings, fixings and fillers. Anything specific is bought in advance and charged at cost.

What if something goes wrong later?

Our workmanship is guaranteed for 12 months. If a repair we did fails, we come back and fix it at no charge.
